The problemLe problème

Bilingual staff are hard to hire, and harder to keep.

A small talent pool

People who write well in both English and French are in demand. You're competing with banks, governments and every company that sells in Quebec.

Weeks before the first reply

Posting the job, interviewing, testing their French, onboarding, training. A new hire can take weeks before they answer customers on their own.

One person, one point of failure

When your only French-speaking rep is sick, on vacation or resigns, your French-speaking customers wait.

QC

Selling to customers in Quebec? Quebec's Charter of the French Language gives consumers the right to be informed and served in French. We give you a French-speaking team without adding to your headcount.

General information, not legal advice.

FAQQuestions fréquentes

Questions we hear often.

Do you use machine translation?

We may use software to work faster, but a fluent bilingual person writes or reviews every reply before it's sent. Your customers get natural French, not a word-for-word translation.

Which French do you write in?

Canadian French by default, the French your customers in Quebec, New Brunswick and Ontario expect. We can adapt for customers in France or elsewhere on request.

Will customers know you're an outside team?

No. We reply under your company name, with your signature and your tone of voice.

What counts as a conversation?

One customer request, including every reply until it's resolved.

How quickly can we start?

About a week after our call. During that week we learn your products, your policies and your most common questions.

Is there a contract?

Plans are month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. The free trial has no contract at all.

How do you protect our customers' data?

Everyone on our team signs a confidentiality agreement. We use only the access we need inside your tools, and we sign a data-processing agreement with you. We follow Canadian privacy law, including PIPEDA and Quebec's Law 25.

Does this make us compliant with Quebec's language law?

We provide your customer service in French, which is a large part of serving Quebec customers in French. For advice on your specific legal obligations, talk to a lawyer.

Where is your team?

We're based in Toronto and work remotely. Every rep passes a written test in English and French before working on your account.
