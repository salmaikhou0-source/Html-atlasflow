# Freelance marketplaces

Companies on these sites post requests and search for help themselves. Replying to a request they posted doesn't need a mailing address. The founder creates the accounts, because they verify identity. The fastest way is "Continue with Google" using bilingualsupportco@gmail.com. Claude writes everything else.

Start with **Fiverr**: it's free to list, and buyers find you through search. It keeps 20% of each sale, and buyers who come through Fiverr must stay on Fiverr (no moving payments off the site). Prices there are in US dollars.

**FAQ:**
