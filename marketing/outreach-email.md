## Rules (Canada's anti-spam law, CASL)
- Only general business addresses that the company publishes on its own website, and only if the site doesn't say it refuses unsolicited messages.
- Every email names the sender and business, gives the business email and website, and includes a mailing address valid for at least 60 days.
- Every email offers an easy way to unsubscribe. Remove anyone who asks, immediately and for good.
- About 15 new emails a day while the Gmail account is new.
