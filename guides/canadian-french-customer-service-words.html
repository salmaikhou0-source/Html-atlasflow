15 Canadian French words for customer service

Updated November 27, 2026 · 4-minute read

French in Canada and French in France share the same grammar, but many everyday words are different. Customers in Quebec, New Brunswick and Ontario notice right away when a reply uses France French or was run through a translation app. Using their words tells them the reply was written for them.

Here are 15 words that come up all the time in customer service, especially for online stores.

Watch out for meal words

The last three rows trip up a lot of businesses. In Quebec, déjeuner is breakfast and dîner is lunch, the opposite of France. If you sell food or deliver meals, a mix-up here can send a customer the wrong delivery time.

Numbers, money and times

  • Money: 25,00 $, not $25.00. The comma replaces the decimal point, and the dollar sign goes after the number.
  • Big numbers: 1 500 $, with a space instead of a comma.
  • Times: 14 h 30, on the 24-hour clock.
  • Dates: le 12 novembre, with no capital letter on the month.
