Privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Who we are

Bilingual Support Co. is a customer support company based in Toronto, Ontario. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

When you use our contact form, we collect the details you enter: your name, work email, company, website, monthly message volume, the plan you're interested in, the tool you use and your message.

We don't use advertising cookies or tracking tools on this website.

Why we collect it

We use this information to reply to you, discuss our services and prepare a proposal. We don't sell it, and we don't share it for marketing.

Service providers

  • Our form provider receives your message and delivers it to our inbox.
  • Our fonts are served by Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when a page loads.

These providers may store or process information outside Canada.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiries for up to 24 months after our last contact with you, then delete them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, or withdraw your consent, at any time.

Person responsible for privacy

Our founder is responsible for protecting personal information at Bilingual Support Co. To make a request, use the contact form and write "Privacy request" in your message.

Our clients' customer data

When we provide support for a client, we handle their customers' information under a written agreement with that client and only on their instructions.
